    YOUR DATA / SAME WORKBENCH

    Bring a race.
    Ask better questions.

    Import one race as CSV. It is sent to this local server for analysis and is not saved by the application. Refreshing restores the demo.

    Download synthetic CSV template ↗
    CSV format & constraintsdriver,lap,lap_time,compound,stint,pit,flag

    Seconds for lap_time; unique driver/lap pairs; pit=1 marks a pit-in lap. Use GREEN, SC, VSC, RED or UNKNOWN. Give every stint its own increasing ID, even for the same compound. Driver codes: uppercase letters, digits, _ or -. Maximum 500 KB, 5,000 rows, 30 drivers. Import only data you are entitled to use.

    The template is fictional, not a historical Grand Prix.
