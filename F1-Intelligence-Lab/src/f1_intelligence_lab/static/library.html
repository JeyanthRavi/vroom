What is loaded?

Races/sprints: all valid available lap timings for all drivers, with conservative quality filtering. Telemetry: one fastest usable accurate, non-deleted lap per available driver. Up to three candidate laps are tried if telemetry is missing.

Shared by Race Lab, ScoutGrid, Replay, Telemetry and numerical ClaimCheck. This does not download FIA decisions or generate incident evidence. Replay remains lap-relative, not simultaneous race positions.
